About §CIRE MAT

§CIRE MAT was developed as a collaboration by Kevin Iu & Jason Turvey, based on Jason's article "How to analyse your own games", published on schemingmind.com and bracknellchessclub.co.uk.

§CIRE MAT stands for:

§
Scilicet (that is to say)
C
Candidate
I
Investigation
R
Rating
E
Evaluation
M
Move
A
Analysis
T
Tool

§CIRE is based on the Latin word scire, which means "to know", and is pronounced the same way, i.e. SKEE-reh.

This program is entirely free. If you think it is good and would like to make a small donation for our efforts, please buy us a coffee here.
